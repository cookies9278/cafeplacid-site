Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Cafe Placid Live Rotator accesses, uses, stores, and protects information obtained through Google and YouTube APIs.

1. Purpose of the Application

Cafe Placid Live Rotator is a private automation tool used by the operator of the Cafe Placid YouTube channel to manage authorized YouTube live broadcasts.

2. Information Accessed

The application may access information made available through the YouTube Data API and related Google OAuth permissions, including information necessary to:

  • Create and manage YouTube live broadcasts
  • Read live broadcast and stream status
  • Update broadcast titles, descriptions, thumbnails, and settings
  • Manage scheduled transitions and live-stream operations

3. How Information Is Used

Google and YouTube account information is used only for operating authorized Cafe Placid YouTube live broadcasts and for performing the automation functions requested by the channel operator.

4. Data Storage

OAuth authorization tokens may be stored locally on the operator's computer so the application can continue authorized operations without requiring a new login for every request.

The application does not operate a public user database and does not intentionally collect personal information from members of the public.

5. Data Sharing

Information obtained through Google or YouTube APIs is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect locally stored authentication credentials and authorization tokens from unauthorized access.

7. Revoking Access

Google account access granted to this application can be revoked at any time through the user's Google Account security settings.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

Cafe Placid Live Rotator's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data handling practices change.

10. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the operator of Cafe Placid through the contact email registered with the application's Google OAuth configuration.
